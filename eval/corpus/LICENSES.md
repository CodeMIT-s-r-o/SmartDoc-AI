# Eval corpus: sources and licenses

The corpus is five Slovak acts in English translation (versions, sources and SHA-256 hashes in
[manifest.json](manifest.json)).

## Why they may be used

Under **§ 5 point b) of the Slovak Copyright Act (Act No. 185/2015 Coll.)**, the text of a legal
regulation is not subject to copyright, and neither is its translation, regardless of whether it
meets the conditions of an original work. All five documents are translations of Slovak acts.

## What the documents are, and are not

- They are **unofficial or informative translations**. Only the Slovak text published in the
  Collection of Laws is legally binding; the data protection act says so on its first page.
- They are **snapshots**: the Labour Code and the travel allowances act as effective in 2025,
  the OSH act as of 2022, the data protection act as adopted, and the cybersecurity act in its
  original 2018 wording (before the NIS 2 amendment). Some provisions have changed since.
- The eval measures whether answers are **faithful to these documents**, not whether they match
  the law in force today. Nothing in this project is legal advice.
